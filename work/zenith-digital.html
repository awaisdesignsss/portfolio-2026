Banking

Zenith Digital

A mobile banking app rebuilt around trust and speed, so everyday money tasks take seconds and big moments feel safe.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Timeline
7 months
Year
2022
Industry
Banking / Mobile

The problem

A dated app where simple tasks were buried and security felt bolted on. People dropped to the branch or call center for things the app should handle.

My role

I led the mobile redesign across onboarding, payments, and account management, with a security team shaping every sensitive flow.

The outcome

A fast home for daily tasks, transfers that confirm clearly, and security moments that reassure instead of interrupt.

+47%

Increase in tasks completed in-app

4.8★

App store rating, up from 3.1

-35%

Calls to support for routine tasks

Challenge

Reading the real problem.

Banking design carries two opposite demands: make the common things effortless, and make the risky things feel deliberate. Lean too far either way and you get an app that’s either careless or exhausting.

Process

From questions to structure.

I mapped the everyday tasks people repeat and the rare ones that scare them, then designed two registers in one system. We pressure-tested the sensitive flows with the security team and real users, tuning confirmations until they reassured without nagging.

Solution

What we shipped.

Zenith puts daily money one tap from home and slows down only where it counts. Confirmations are clear, security reads as care rather than friction, and the whole app feels quick without ever feeling loose.
