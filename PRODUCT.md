# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Design leaders, hiring managers, and potential clients evaluating M. Awais as a UX/UI & Product Designer. They arrive from LinkedIn, Dribbble, or referrals, scanning for craft quality and design sensibility within seconds.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio site for M. Awais. Communicates design expertise through the site itself: the portfolio IS the proof of skill. Success = a visitor who stays, explores work, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, confident, precise

## Anti-references
- Generic template portfolios (Wix/Squarespace defaults)
- Overly minimal "white box with Helvetica" portfolios
- Cluttered agency sites with too many CTAs
- AI-generated landing pages with gradient text and glass cards everywhere

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell**: the site's craft speaks louder than any copy
- **Cinematic presence**: dark, editorial, photography-forward; every viewport is a composed frame
- **Quiet confidence**: premium feel without trying too hard; let the work breathe
- **Precision over decoration**: every pixel intentional, no filler elements

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion alternatives for all animations. Sufficient contrast for all text on dark backgrounds.
